2. Session rules

Cancellations

  1. Any cancellation must be made at least 48 hours before the session start time.
  2. Last-minute cancellations affect the game for everyone, so please be mindful.
  3. Any cancellation within 24 hours of the session will be treated as a No-Show, and the session fee will be non-refundable.

Replacements

  1. Players who cancel may arrange their own replacement and settle payment directly, outside of the admin.
  2. No free play for replacement players. All replacements must be willing to pay.

Payments

  1. The payment per player will be finalised 24 hours before the session, based on the total number of confirmed players.
  2. All payments must be made at least 24 hours before the session start time.

Exception: kids (under 16)

Kids are allowed to play only if there are spaces available due to cancellations, or if existing players are happy to include them in the game. These rules do not otherwise apply to kids.

3. Group chat rules

  1. Please be polite and respectful at all times.
  2. No hate speech, and no political or religious debates in the group.
  3. Keep the chat friendly. Have a laugh and enjoy healthy play.
  4. No personal attacks or arguments.
  5. No business promotion or advertising.
  6. No messages after 11:00 pm, unless it is urgent or admin-related.
  7. The admin reserves the right to delete any messages that are inappropriate or off-topic.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation 😊

4. Frequently asked questions

When and where are the sessions?

Thursday evenings from 9:00 pm to 10:00 pm, and Sunday evenings from 7:00 pm to 8:00 pm, at Oasis Academy Salford, King William St, Salford, M50 3UQ.

See the full details

How do I join a session?

Open the session from the home page, type your name, pick it from the list and press Join. If there is a free spot you are confirmed straight away.

What happens if the session is full?

You are added to the waiting list. When a spot opens up it goes to the people already waiting first, and the admin moves players up from the waiting list. Check back on the session page to see if you have been confirmed.

How do I cancel?

Open the session and press the ✕ next to your name. Please do this at least 48 hours before the session starts.

Within 24 hours of the start the ✕ is locked and shows a padlock instead. After that point only the admin can remove a name, and the cancellation counts as a No-Show.

What if I have to cancel at the last minute?

Any cancellation within 24 hours of the session is treated as a No-Show and the session fee is non-refundable.

The best option is to find a replacement yourself (see the next question).

Can I send someone in my place?

Yes. You can arrange your own replacement and settle payment with them directly, outside of the admin. There is no free play for replacements, so they must be willing to pay.

How much do I pay, and when?

The amount per player is finalised 24 hours before the session, based on the number of confirmed players. All payments must be made at least 24 hours before the session starts.

How do I show that I have paid?

After you have sent your fee, press Mark Paid next to your name on the session page. It then shows ✓ Paid.

Pressed it by mistake? Tap ✓ Paid again to switch back to unpaid.

I have already paid but cannot make it. What do I do?

While your name is marked as Paid you cannot remove yourself from the list. Either switch it back to unpaid first, or contact the admin to adjust your payment.

If your place was covered by a prepaid bundle, please contact the admin. Remember the cancellation timings in the session rules still apply.

Can my child play?

Kids under 16 can play only if there are spaces available because of cancellations, or if the existing players are happy to include them in the game. The other session rules do not otherwise apply to kids.

What are the rules for the group chat?

Be polite and respectful, keep it friendly, and no hate speech, personal attacks, arguments, business promotion, or political or religious debates. Please do not message after 11:00 pm unless it is urgent or admin-related.

Read the full group chat rules

Who do I contact if something is wrong?

Message the admin through the group chat. This covers things like adjusting a payment, removing your name inside the 24-hour lock, or anything that looks wrong on the session page.
